TallyKeeper: Driving Hours — Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

TallyKeeper helps families log supervised practice driving hours. It is built so that your log stays with you. We do not run servers, we do not collect your data, and we do not track you.

What the app stores

Information you enter is stored on your device:

  • Driver names, permit dates and state
  • Supervisor names, nicknames and license numbers
  • Drives: date, time, duration, day and night minutes, weather, road types, skills practiced, notes, and an approximate location (about 1 km) used to calculate sunrise and sunset

Family sharing through iCloud

If you choose to share a family log, your entries are stored in your own private iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit and shared only with the people you invite. We cannot see or access this data. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy. You can stop sharing at any time from the app or from iCloud settings.

Location

With your permission, the app reads your approximate location once when you log a drive, so it can tell how much of the drive happened at night. Your route is never tracked, and location is not used in the background.

Microphone, speech and photos

When you describe a drive out loud, speech is transcribed on your device. When you import a photo of a paper log, text is read from the image on your device. When available, Apple Intelligence on your device helps turn your words into a log entry. Audio, transcripts and photos are not sent to us or to any third party.

Purchases

The optional Family purchase is handled entirely by the App Store. We do not receive your payment information.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking
  • No selling or sharing of your information
  • No collection of data by us

Exports

When you export your log (for example, to fill in your state’s practice log form), the file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to send it.

Deleting your data

Deleting an entry in the app removes it from your device and, if you share a family log, from your iCloud. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device.

Children

TallyKeeper is intended for use by families with a supervising adult. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because we do not collect personal information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
